Sahara Desert, Morocco

A night in the heart of the Sahara

Camels in, quad bikes out, and a night by the campfire somewhere in between. This is the trip where the desert stopped being a postcard and became real.

  • 🐪 Camel trek
  • 🏂 Sandboarding
  • ⛺ Desert camp
  • 🔥 Campfire
  • 🏍️ Quad bikes
The way in

Into the desert by camel

We headed for the middle of the Sahara the old-fashioned way: on camelback, swaying over the dunes with nothing but sand in every direction.

Along the way

Sandboarding the dunes

On the way, we stopped to go sandboarding: climbing to the top of a dune and sliding all the way back down. That's me in the video.

Nightfall

A camp in the middle of nowhere

Our home for the night was a tented camp right in the heart of the Sahara, with every facility you could need, surrounded by nothing but dunes.

After dark

Dinner, a campfire, and good company

We had dinner at the camp, then gathered around the campfire, chit-chatting with my fellow travellers under the desert sky.

The way out

Back to land by quad bike

Camels brought us in, but we went out a lot faster: on quad bikes, racing across the dunes and kicking up sand all the way back to land.
