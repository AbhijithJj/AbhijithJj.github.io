County Wicklow

Three trips into Wicklow

  • 🥾 20 km+ hike
  • 🏞️ Upper Lake
  • 🪵 Wooden path
  • ⛏️ Mining history
  • 🌅 Greystones sunset
  • 🍛 Dinner at Daata
Trip one

Glendalough, more than 20 km

The Glendalough hike was more than 20 km and long, but I really enjoyed it: the beautiful Upper Lake, the wooden path across the hills, the pines and all.

Trip one

A valley that once mined

I also learned that Glendalough was once a stronghold for mining, long before it became a place for hikers.

Trip two

Bray Head to Greystones

On another day I hiked up Bray Head, then went on to Greystones: a beautiful harbour, and a good sunset to end the day.

Trip two

Dinner at Daata

After that we went to Daata and had a wholesome dinner.

Trip three

Into the Wicklow Mountains

My third trip took me into the Wicklow Mountains.

More than 20 km, and I’d walk every one of them again.
