County Kildare

Village lanes, sweet treats, and fairy lights at dusk.

Kildare was a slow wander through pretty shopping lanes, past quirky sculptures and colourful shopfronts, with a crêpe in hand, until the trees lit up with fairy lights as the evening rolled in.

Come for the shops, stay for the glow when the lights come on.
