Naples, Italy

A conference in Naples

I came to Naples for a conference. A suit, a badge, a marble hall full of slides and my turn at the podium, with plenty of Neapolitan food in between.

Day one, early

Suiting up

Conference day started early: suit on, a quick breakfast at a street café, and a walk through Naples as the city woke up.

The conference

Talks in a marble hall

The sessions ran in a hall that looked more like a cathedral than a conference room: marble walls, a gilded dome, and a big screen of slides in the middle of it all.

The podium

My turn

And then it was my turn at the podium, in front of that same hall.

Between sessions

Lunches, coffee and an orange-tree courtyard

The breaks were half the fun: standing lunches and wine with other attendees, a proper Neapolitan coffee, and a courtyard full of orange trees.

After hours

Dinner around a long table

Evenings ended around a long table with a big crowd.

Naples

Eating my way through Naples

With the conference done, it was time to explore the cuisine of Naples.

Naples

Wandering the old streets

And in between meals, the streets: cloisters, nativity stalls, Maradona everywhere, and a sunset over the piazza.
