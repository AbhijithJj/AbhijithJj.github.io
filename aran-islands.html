Inis Mór, Aran Islands

A day on two wheels on the Aran Islands

  • 🚌 Bus to Galway
  • ⛴️ Aran Island Ferries
  • 🚲 Bike rental
  • 🏰 Ruins and cliffs
  • 🦙 Alpacas and horses
  • 🕳️ The Wormhole
Getting there

Bus, bus, ferry

We took the bus from Dublin to Galway, another bus on to Rossaveal, and then the ferry across to the Aran Islands with Aran Island Ferries.

On the island

Two wheels and a whole island

On the island we rented bicycles to roam around. Stone-walled lanes, open sky and the sea never far away.

First stops

Ruins, cliffs and stones

First we saw a ruined castle, then the cliffs, and ruined stones scattered across the island, some of them piled into towers by visitors.

Along the way

Alpacas, horses and seals

All the way round we kept meeting animals: alpacas, horses, and even seals.

The highlight

The Wormhole

Then we went to see the Wormhole, and it was spectacular. Some people were even jumping into it from a height. Scary and eye-pleasing at the same time (that’s the video at the top).

Heading home

Racing the last ferry

The last ferry was in the evening, so with time running out we made it back to the ferry station before 6, then caught the train home to Dublin.

Scary and eye-pleasing at the same time.
