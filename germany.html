Germany memories

High ridges, pine forests, and the quiet thrill of the Bavarian Alps.

Germany surprised me with its scale and stillness. Alpine peaks gave way to quiet trails, and every overlook felt like a reward for the climb.

A country of crisp air, old towns, sweeping ridgelines, and mornings that start with mist in the valleys.

The route

West to the Alps, then back up north

Aachen, Stuttgart, Munich, the Neuschwanstein castle country, Rosenheim, then Frankfurt — six stops across the country.
