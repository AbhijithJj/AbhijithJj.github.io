Cliffs of Moher, west of Ireland

Holding on at the Cliffs of Moher

It was just the Cliffs of Moher on this trip, but it was worth it: a wild wind, a violent sea and a view that felt surreal.

  • 🚌 7 AM from Dublin
  • 🌬️ Wild wind
  • 🌊 Violent sea
  • 🛍️ Souvenir shops
  • ⚡ Harry Potter
7 AM to 11 AM

Dublin at seven, the cliffs by eleven

I went with a paid sightseeing tour that left Dublin at 7 in the morning, and we reached the Cliffs of Moher around 11.

The weather

Wind that steals phones

It was so windy that day that my phone literally flew out of my hand. Luckily, nothing happened to it. I saw some old grannies struggling against the wind, while some kids were having the time of their lives playing with it.

The view

Cliffs, sea and the path

The whole line of cliffs, the violent sea and the path along the edge: everything was surreal.

Before leaving

Tiny memorabilia shops

There were some tiny memorabilia shops too, tucked into the hillside.

Movie magic

A little Harry Potter

And I heard that a small scene from Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince was shot around here.

The wind tried to take my phone. The cliffs took my breath.
