Downpatrick Head, County Mayo

Otherworldly Downpatrick Head

I came for one rock in the sea. I left having seen a violent ocean, layered cliffs, centuries of history, endless green and a wind that never stopped.

  • 🪨 Dún Briste
  • 🌊 A violent sea
  • ⛰️ Layered cliffs
  • 📜 History
  • 🌿 Green and wind
What I came for

Just a rock in the sea, I thought

I went to Downpatrick Head expecting one thing: that pie-like stack of stone standing alone in the sea, Dún Briste. It was there, and it was stunning. But Downpatrick turned out to be so much more than that.

The sea

A violent sea

The sea was violent, crashing and churning against the cliffs and boiling up through the openings in the rock below.

The land

Hills, stone and layers

Then there was the land itself: different kinds of hills and headlands, and stone stacked in layers like the pages of a book.

The past

The history of Downpatrick

The headland carries its history with it, from a statue of St Patrick to a wartime lookout with an old EIRE 64 sign still marking the spot.

The feeling

The greenery and the wind

And around all of it, the brightest green grass and a wind that never let up. It felt otherworldly.

I expected a rock in the sea. I found another world.
