County Westmeath

Go-karting, and losing to the kids

Dublin only had indoor tracks and we wanted to race outdoors, so we headed to Westmeath. Fun, fast, and humbling.

  • 🏎️ Outdoor go-karting
  • 🍳 Breakfast first
  • 🧒 Out-driven by kids
  • 😅 Worst driver: me
The plan

Outdoor or nothing

Westmeath was just for go-karting. We wanted outdoor go-karting, and Dublin only had indoor tracks, so Westmeath was the best option. We had our breakfast first, then went to try our luck.

On the track

Trying our luck

Then it was helmets on and out onto the track. It was so much fun.

The verdict

Out-driven by kids

A lot of kids joined us, and they were literally toying with those karts. I found out I was the one who drove the worst. Anyway, it was a new experience.

Worst driver on the track. Still a new experience.
