Dublin, three years

Dublin, my three-year roller coaster

A master’s at TU Dublin, a string of apartments, jobs from waiter and security guard to teaching assistant and analyst, and everything in between. This is where most of my Ireland happened.

The reason

A master’s at TU Dublin

Dublin is where I spent most of my time in Ireland: three years in all. It started with my master’s at TU Dublin, with long days of lectures, laptops and coffee.

Home

The apartments

Home changed a few times over those three years, but every apartment came with its own view, its own sunsets and its own stories.

The hustle

Waiter, security guard, teaching assistant, analyst

To get through it I did all kinds of jobs: waiter, security guard, teaching assistant and, eventually, analyst. It was a roller-coaster ride.

Nine to five

Office life

And then came office life: my own badge, a desk with two screens, little figurines guarding the monitor, the odd VR headset to try out and a bike park full of commuters.

Spring

Parks and cherry blossoms

Dublin’s parks were my escape, especially in spring, when the cherry blossoms came out.

Weekends

Football

Weekends meant football, whether a ball in the park or a game with friends.

Eating out

Food

Food was its own adventure: wings on a terrace, dessert under purple lights and long dinners with friends.

Nights out

Pubs and the city

And of course, the pubs: Temple Bar all lit up, rows of taps, and nights wandering the city.

Three years, a master’s, four kinds of jobs, and a city that became home.
