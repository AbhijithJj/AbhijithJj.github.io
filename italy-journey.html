Naples

Landing for the conference

It started when I landed in Naples to attend a conference: badge on, in a marble hall full of talks.

Read the full conference story →

Naples

Exploring Naples

With the conference done, I explored Naples: pizza, coffee in gilded cafés, nativity-stall streets and Maradona on every wall.

Rome

On to Rome

Then I moved on to Rome and explored it: the Colosseum inside and out, the Forum’s ruins and arches, the Pantheon, and the fountains.

Pisa

Pisa, by day and by night

After Rome I took the train towards Florence, on my way to Pisa, bunked down in a hostel dorm, and went to see the tower that really does lean.

Milan

Ending in Milan

And from Pisa I went on to Milan, where the Duomo was lit up for Christmas. The last stop was Bergamo airport, before my flight to Paris.
