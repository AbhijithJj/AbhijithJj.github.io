Cappadocia, Turkey

Chasing sunrise over the fairy chimneys

Cappadocia at dawn is something you have to see to believe. One very early morning, a sky full of balloons, and my favourite memory from Turkey.

  • ⏰ 4:00 AM wake-up
  • 🎈 Hot air balloons
  • 🌅 Sunrise
4:00 AM

The alarm nobody wants

The alarm went off at 4 in the morning. Painful? Absolutely. But in Cappadocia, the earlier you're up, the better the view — and I wasn't going to miss it.

Before sunrise

Finding the perfect spot

We'd booked a hot air balloon tour, and the crew knew exactly where to take us: the best possible spot to watch the balloons, with the majestic fairy chimneys stretching out behind us as the first sunlight crept over the valley.

Liftoff

Watching the balloons come alive

Up close, the whole thing is mesmerising. I watched the balloons slowly being pumped up, swelling from flat fabric into giants, and the passengers climbing into their baskets one by one before they lifted off the ground.

Golden hour

A sky full of balloons

Then the sky filled up. So many hot air balloons, floating wherever the wind carried them, drifting over the fairy chimneys as the sun lit everything up in gold. I just stood there and took it all in.

Waking up at 4 AM has never been this worth it.
