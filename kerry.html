County Kerry

A road trip around the Ring of Kerry

  • 🚗 Road trip

  • 🏖️ Ballybunion
  • 🌄 Ring of Kerry

10 AM

Dublin to Tralee

We left Dublin by car at 10 in the morning, and our first stop was Tralee for breakfast.

Afternoon

Ballybunion

From Tralee we went on to Ballybunion: green dunes, a ruined castle on the headland and the beach below.

Evening

The beautiful Ring of Kerry

Then we enjoyed the Ring of Kerry, and it was so beautiful. I didn’t click many pictures, and honestly, the ones I did take don’t do it justice.

That night

Dinner and a night’s stay

We had a quality time there: dinner together, and we stayed over for the night.

Next morning

Torc Waterfall, then on to Cork

The next day we covered Torc Waterfall before heading on to Cork.

The pictures don’t do it justice. You just have to go.
