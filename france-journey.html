Paris, at Christmas

One night in Paris

  • 🏔️ Over the Alps
  • 🛏️ Hostel dorm
  • 🗼 Eiffel Tower
  • 🏛️ Arc de Triomphe
  • 🎄 Christmas lights
  • 🛫 Back to Dublin
Evening

Into Paris over the Alps

Paris was one quality night. Before landing, the French Alps rolled past under the wing, and I reached Paris around evening.

Check-in

Six strangers and a bunk

I crashed in a hostel, sharing the dorm with six strangers.

Night

The Eiffel Tower, all lit up

Then I started exploring Paris by night, starting with the Eiffel Tower, glowing gold against the dark sky.

Later that night

The Arc de Triomphe

From there, on to the Arc de Triomphe, standing tall over the traffic swirling around it.

Christmas time

Paris, lit for Christmas

It was Christmas time, so the whole city was lit up: avenues of glowing trees, sparkling shop windows and buildings dressed in lights.

Heading home

Back to Dublin

And just like that, the night was over: passport and boarding pass in hand, it was time to fly back to Dublin.
