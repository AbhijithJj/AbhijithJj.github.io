County Sligo, Ireland

Three trips to Sligo

  • 🚆 Dublin to Sligo
  • 🏰 Classiebawn Castle
  • ⛰️ Benbulben
  • 🐑 Sheep everywhere
  • 🕳️ Keash Caves
  • 🐴 Horses & donkeys
  • 💧 Devil’s Chimney
Trip one

Train, bus, and a castle by the sea

I’ve been to Sligo three times. The first time, I took the train from Dublin to Sligo, then a bus to Mullaghmore, and walked towards the beautiful Classiebawn Castle, with Benbulben all around.

Trip one

Sheep, Benbulben and the sea

Walking from Mullaghmore to Classiebawn, we saw a lot of beautiful sheep, and a beautiful sea.

Trip two

Off the train early for Keash

Next we went to the Keash Caves. We got off the train at the stop before Sligo and walked towards the caves. There wasn’t any footpath that way, but we made it.

Trip two

A grandpa, horses and donkeys

On the way back we saw a lot of animals at people’s homes: horses, donkeys and sheep. One grandpa even invited us into his home to show them to us.

Trip three

Gleniff Horseshoe and the Devil’s Chimney

On the third trip we covered the Gleniff Horseshoe and the Devil’s Chimney.
