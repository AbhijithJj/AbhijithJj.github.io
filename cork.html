Afternoon

A short stint in Cork

Cork was a short stint. We just roamed around the city centre and had our lunch.

Cobh

A bucket-list stop

Then we went to Cobh, which had been on my Irish bucket list for a long time. First we visited the huge cathedral, and the interior was surreal.

The view

The Deck of Cards

Then we went looking for the best view of the Deck of Cards, the row of colourful houses stacked down the steep hill.

The history

Where ships docked

I also got to know Cobh’s docking history, from the Titanic to the many other ships that called here.

Cobh had been on my list for years. It didn’t disappoint.
